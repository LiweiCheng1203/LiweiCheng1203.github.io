We build a multimodal large model analysis framework for space target situational awareness that fuses **optical images, ISAR images, attitude, and trajectory information**, achieving semantic-level perception and interpretable recognition. The framework includes an LLM + SAM pipeline for satellite body and solar-panel extraction, and multimodal SFT for maneuver-type recognition with interpretable rationale generation.
