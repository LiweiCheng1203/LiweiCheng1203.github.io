We construct a multilingual in-image text editing benchmark covering **12 languages and 3,600 samples**, design a same-background multilingual data construction method, and propose VLM-based semantic evaluation metrics. Our study reveals and verifies that the in-image text editing capability of multimodal models **degrades significantly in low-resource languages**, providing a reference for cross-lingual alignment and fine-tuning of multimodal large models.
