I am **Liwei Cheng (程立伟)**, an undergraduate student in the **Yongtan Honors Class** (Future Technology College / Honors School) at **Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT)**, majoring in Electronic Information Engineering (expected graduation: June 2027).

My research interest centers on **embodied AI and agent systems in complex real-world environments**, with a particular focus on how intelligent systems achieve **effective perception and reliable decision-making**. I am seeking a PhD (or Master's) position in embodied AI / agents starting Fall 2027, and I am always happy to chat about potential opportunities and collaborations.

Research Interests
======
* Embodied AI and agent systems
* Vision-language models (VLMs) and multimodal large models
* Vision-and-language navigation (VLN)
* Spatial reasoning and world models

Research Experience
======
* **Cross-lingual degradation of in-image text editing in multimodal models** (Aug 2025 – Mar 2026)  
  Built a multilingual in-image text editing benchmark (12 languages, 3,600 samples) with VLM-based semantic evaluation, revealing significant degradation in low-resource languages. *First-author paper under review at EMNLP.*

* **Optical–ISAR multimodal large model for space situational awareness** (Apr 2026 – May 2026)  
  Built a multimodal framework fusing optical/ISAR images with attitude and trajectory information for interpretable space target recognition. *First-author EI conference paper under review (CIE).*

* **Cross-platform universal autonomous navigation with VLN models** (Mar 2026 – present)  
  Training a VLN foundation model (StarVLA) that adapts a single model to UAVs, UGVs, and mobile robots; lead the standardized evaluation pipeline covering 10+ navigation benchmarks.

* **Dynamic spatial reasoning VLMs via world-model distillation** (Dec 2025 – Apr 2026)  
  World model as training-time teacher with bidirectional SFT distillation and task-aware GRPO, improving dynamic spatial reasoning by ~15 points on average across four benchmarks.
