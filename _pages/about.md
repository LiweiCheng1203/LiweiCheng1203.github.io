My research interest centers on **embodied AI and agent systems in complex real-world environments**, with a particular focus on how intelligent systems achieve **effective perception and reliable decision-making**. Research Experience
======
* **Cross-lingual degradation of in-image text editing in multimodal models** (Aug 2025 – Mar 2026)  
  Built a multilingual in-image text editing benchmark covering **12 languages and 3,600 samples**; designed the construction method for same-background multilingual data and proposed VLM-based semantic evaluation metrics, revealing significant degradation of in-image text editing in low-resource languages. *First-author paper under review at EMNLP.*

* **Optical–ISAR multimodal large model for space situational awareness** (Apr 2026 – May 2026)  
  Developed a multimodal analysis framework fusing optical images, ISAR images, attitude, and trajectory information. Built the LLM + SAM pipeline for satellite body and solar-panel extraction, and designed multimodal SFT for maneuver-type recognition with interpretable rationales. *First-author EI conference paper under review (CIE).*

* **Cross-platform universal autonomous navigation with VLN models** (Mar 2026 – present)  
  Based on the StarVLA framework, training a vision-language navigation foundation model that adapts a **single model to UAVs, UGVs, and mobile robots**, achieving SOTA on multiple benchmarks. I lead the evaluation module: a standardized, automated, and reusable evaluation pipeline covering **10+ mainstream navigation benchmarks** including R2R.

* **Dynamic spatial reasoning VLMs via world-model distillation** (Dec 2025 – Apr 2026)  
  Used a world model as a training-time teacher to generate post-action viewpoint-shift data; combined forward/inverse bidirectional SFT distillation with task-aware GRPO, improving dynamic spatial reasoning by **~15 points on average across four benchmarks** including SAT-Real. I was responsible for viewpoint-shift data generation and the bidirectional-supervision QA design.
